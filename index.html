🚀 What Is RcloneExplorer?

RcloneExplorer is a desktop application that puts a clean, easy-to-use window in front of rclone — a powerful tool that connects to more than 70 cloud storage services. Instead of typing commands, you simply click on your files and folders the same way you would in File Explorer on Windows.

If you have ever kept files in more than one place — a Google Drive account for work, a personal OneDrive, a Dropbox folder shared with family, or an archive sitting in pCloud or Amazon S3 — you already know the frustration of switching between browser tabs and apps. RcloneExplorer brings all of those places together in a single screen so you can see what you have, move things around, and keep everything up to date.

It is portable, which means it does not clutter your computer with a long installation process. You keep it in one folder, open it when you need it, and close it when you are done. Your cloud accounts, your settings, and your file lists live in that same tidy location, so you always know where everything is.

✨ Key Features You Will Use Every Day

  • Browse every cloud in one place. Open a side-by-side view of two different storage providers and see your folders just like on your own hard drive.
  • Drag-and-drop transfers. Move a folder from Google Drive to OneDrive, or copy photos from your PC to pCloud, without ever opening a web browser.
  • One-way and two-way sync. Keep a local folder and a cloud folder matching each other automatically, or make a cloud folder mirror a local one.
  • Backup-friendly workflow. Copy important folders to a second cloud so a single outage or accident never costs you your files.
  • Encrypted storage support. If you already use encrypted remotes, RcloneExplorer recognises them and works with them like any other folder.
  • Clear progress and results. See transfer speed, file counts, and any skipped files in plain language instead of scrolling through technical logs.
  • Saved configurations. Set up each cloud account once and it stays ready for next time.
  • Mount a cloud as a drive. Work with remote files as though they were on a lettered drive on your own computer.

🖱️ Your First Five Minutes

Once the application is open, the window is split into simple areas. On one side you choose where you want to look, and in the middle you see your folders and files. Everything is designed so that a first-time user can get to work without reading a manual.

  1. Pick a cloud. Click the name of the account you connected, such as Google Drive or OneDrive.
  2. Look around. Double-click folders to open them. The path at the top always shows exactly where you are.
  3. Select what you need. Click a single file, or hold the Shift key to select a range of files.
  4. Choose your destination. Switch the other side of the window to a different cloud or to a folder on your PC.
  5. Press the transfer button. A progress bar shows how much is left. You can keep browsing while it works.

There is no wrong move to make here. Browsing and selecting files never changes anything on your cloud accounts — files only move or change when you deliberately start a copy, move, or sync task.

☁️ Cloud Services You Can Connect

RcloneExplorer supports the same wide range of providers as rclone itself, which means more than 70 services are available to you. The most popular ones for everyday users include:

Google Drive OneDrive Dropbox pCloud Amazon S3 Backblaze B2 Mega Box Google Photos WebDAV SFTP FTP Microsoft Azure Blob Cloudflare R2 Wasabi Yandex Disk

You can connect as many accounts as you like, including two separate accounts on the same service — for example, a work Google Drive and a personal Google Drive. They appear as separate entries in your list, so files never get mixed up by accident.

🔄 Transfer, Copy, and Sync — What Is the Difference?

Three words appear throughout the app, and they mean slightly different things. Understanding them takes about thirty seconds and prevents most beginner mistakes.

  • Copy puts a duplicate of your selected files at the destination. The originals stay exactly where they were. This is the safest option and a good default for most people.
  • Move takes the files from the source and places them at the destination. Use this when you are tidying up and do not want two copies.
  • Sync makes the destination match the source. Files that exist only at the destination may be removed so the two sides look identical. Read the confirmation message before you accept.

If you are ever unsure, choose copy. You can always delete the extra copy afterwards, but you cannot undo a file that was removed by mistake.

🔒 Is It Safe to Use?

Yes. RcloneExplorer does not run its own cloud servers and does not hold your files in the middle. Your data moves directly between your computer and the storage provider you signed in to, using that provider's own secure connection.

Your account details and settings are stored on your own machine, in the folder where you keep RcloneExplorer. Nothing is uploaded to a third party. When you are finished, you can simply close the app — there is no background service left running.
